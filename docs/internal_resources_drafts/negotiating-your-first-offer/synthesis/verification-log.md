## 1. Northeastern-specific constraints (co-op timing, international students)

**Brief pending:** Fact-check NEU co-op office timing, international work authorization vs offer deadlines; author note: “indeed do double check.”

### Verified — Co-op offer acknowledgment (university-wide marketing / process page)

Northeastern’s official “The co-op process, from start to finish” states that students should **acknowledge all offers within 48 hours**, work with the co-op coordinator because **once you accept a position, you’re committed**, and international students should **check for any visa requirements**.

- Source: [The co-op process, from start to finish](https://www.northeastern.edu/the-co-op-process-from-start-to-finish/) (Northeastern University; retrieved 2026-05-10), section “5. Accepting an offer.”

**Draft implication:** The brief’s “first 24–72 hours” safe sequence should be reconciled with this **48-hour acknowledgment** norm for co-op (not necessarily identical for every non–co-op internship, but NEU-shaped readers on co-op should see 48 hours as the hard university messaging).

### Verified — F-1 CPT and offer / employment changes (OGS)

From Northeastern Office of Global Services, **Curricular Practical Training (CPT)**:

- CPT is required for every co-op / internship / approved experiential opportunity; authorization is **employer, location, and date-specific**.
- **CPT authorization must be obtained before the intended start** of the opportunity (same page: authorization before start; I-20 lists work only within authorized start/end dates).
- OGS reviews completed CPT e-form submissions **within 15 business days** after the secondary approver submits to OGS.
- **Extensions, cancellations, or changes** to authorized CPT require approval from **academic advisor/co-op coordinator and OGS** first.

- Source: [F-1 Curricular Practical Training](https://international.northeastern.edu/ogs/employment/curricular-practical-training-cpt-f1/) (Northeastern OGS; retrieved 2026-05-10), sections “Understanding CPT,” “Application Process,” “Updating or changing your CPT authorization.”

**Draft implication:** Negotiating **start date, employer, location, or full-time vs part-time hours** after a CPT request exists is not “just between you and the recruiter”—it triggers **CPT update** workflows and coordinator + OGS involvement. Article prose should tell international readers to **loop in co-op coordinator and OGS early** when offer terms might change.

### Verified — Khoury supplement (extensions + supplemental work)

Khoury undergraduate co-op policies (adds to catalog policy):

- **Co-op extensions** into summer: request permission **in writing from the co-op coordinator at least two weeks** before the extension; international students need **I-20 dates updated** via **CPT Update Form** on OGS; working without proper authorization risks status violation.
- **Supplemental work while on co-op:** international students must check with **OGS** before accepting work outside co-op.

- Source: [Undergraduate Co-op Policies — Khoury College](https://www.khoury.northeastern.edu/undergraduate-co-op-policies/) (retrieved 2026-05-10), sections “Supplemental work while on co-op” and “Back-to-back co-ops and co-op extensions.”

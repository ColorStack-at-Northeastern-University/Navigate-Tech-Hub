# Hackathon Preparation System

## Editor meta

- Silent-read word budget: about 900–1,100 words.
- Voice: `.cursor/rules/navigate-writing.mdc`.
- Volatility **medium**: event formats differ; avoid “all hackathons require X.”

## Opening (replace with friction-led prose)

You can brute-force a hackathon on caffeine and panic. A light system saves your team from collapsing at hour eighteen over scope fights.

## Pre-event: team contracts

- Roles flexible, decisions fast: who breaks ties.
- Skill map: who can deploy, who can demo, who can UI.

## Idea vetting in thirty minutes

- Feasibility vs wow: matrix you can reuse.
- What to build when nobody has a “big idea.”

## Repo and tooling setup before doors open

- Template repo, blank deploy pipeline, shared Figma or wireframe habit.

## Time-boxing the build

- Milestones at hours 6, 12, 20, whatever matches your event.
- Sleep plan stated out loud before coding starts.

## Integration moments

- When frontend meets backend without a three-hour merge fight.

## Submission buffer

- Video, form fields, upload size limits: read the devpost page early.

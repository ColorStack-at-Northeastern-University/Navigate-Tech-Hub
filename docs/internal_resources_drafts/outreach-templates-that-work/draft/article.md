## Opening (replace with friction-led prose)

Bad outreach is long, self-centered, and impossible to forward. Good outreach makes it easy for a busy person to say yes to a small ask.

## Structure every message needs

- Who you are in one line, why them specifically, one concrete ask, easy out.

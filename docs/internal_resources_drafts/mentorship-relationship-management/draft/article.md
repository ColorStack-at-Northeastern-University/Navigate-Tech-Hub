# How to Build and Maintain Mentor Relationships

## Editor meta

- Silent-read word budget: about 900–1,100 words.
- Voice: `.cursor/rules/navigate-writing.mdc`.
- Volatility **low**.

## Opening (replace with friction-led prose)

Mentors do not owe you momentum. They respond when you make it easy to help and hard to feel used.

## What mentors are actually for

- Perspective, introductions you earned, blunt reads on blind spots.
- What they are not: on-demand homework help or decision-making by proxy.

## First conversation: small ask, clear constraint

- Time box, prep sent the night before, agenda in three bullets.

## Cadence without spam

- Update emails with progress since last touch, specific question at the bottom.

## Gratitude that does not read performative

- What changed because of their advice; one line is enough.

## Drifting and ending well

- When to release someone gently, how to say thank you on the way out.

## Power dynamics and boundaries

- Professional tone, no emotional dumping, respect response delays.

## 3. Additional verification on Tier-1 GEMs

These were not flagged in the brief but were re-checked because they are load-bearing for the article.

### 3.1 GEM: Same-day/next-day follow-up; close-the-loop after acting; quarterly value cadence
**Status:** Verified (Micromentor primary source)
**Source:** https://micromentor.org/blog/how-do-you-keep-mentors-engaged-after-a-first-meeting
**Confirmed:** thank-you email immediately after the meeting that names *what* was useful and *what next steps* the mentee will take; second follow-up after implementing advice with results; calendar reminder to deliver value every 90 days (offering skills, intros, ideas, relevant resources). This is the source of the "three-move follow-up loop" and the quarterly cadence default in the brief.

### 3.2 GEM: Cadence ("formal programs sometimes publish month/quarter patterns")
**Status:** Verified (MENTOR — Elements of Effective Practice for Mentoring, 4th ed.)
**Source:** https://eepm.mentoring.org/element/supporting-mentoring-relationships/
**Confirmed structure for *formal* programs (illustrative, not a rule for informal coffee chats):** quick monthly touch-points with each participant during the first year; in-depth support meetings monthly during the first quarter of the relationship, then at least once per quarter thereafter; programs are encouraged to maintain at least quarterly contact even after relationships are well-established.
**Action for draft:** Safe to anchor the "monthly early, quarterly later" default in MENTOR's published guidance — but framed as a benchmark from formal mentoring programs, not a universal rule. Do not present this as Code2040 or MLT's published cadence specifically.

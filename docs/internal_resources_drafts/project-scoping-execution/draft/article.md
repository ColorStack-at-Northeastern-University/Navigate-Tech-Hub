# Project Scoping and Execution in 6 Weeks

## Editor meta

- Silent-read word budget: about 900–1,100 words.
- Voice: `.cursor/rules/navigate-writing.mdc`.
- Volatility **medium**: tooling references may age; keep milestones timeless.

## Opening (replace with friction-led prose)

Six weeks sounds long until midterms eat two of them. Scope for the calendar you have, not the calendar you wish for.

## Week 0: one sentence goal + non-goals

- “Ship” means ______ for this project.
- Explicit list of what you are not building.

## Milestone map (suggested spine)

- Week 1: skeleton and manual demo path.
- Week 2–3: core user loop.
- Week 4: hardest integration.
- Week 5: polish, tests, bugs.
- Week 6: docs, deploy, retrospective.

## Cutting scope without guilt

- Feature triage when the integration week slips.
- What to delete before you add “just one more thing.”

## Solo vs team execution

- Interfaces between teammates: who owns merge conflicts, who owns deploy.
- **Your bullets:** lightweight rituals (15-min standup, shared board).

## Done definition

- Checklist: deploy, readme, demo video or gif, retrospective note for interviews.

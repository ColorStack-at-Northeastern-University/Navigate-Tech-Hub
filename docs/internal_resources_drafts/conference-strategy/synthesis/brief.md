## KEY FINDINGS

**[GEM]** **Upload resume to the conference talent portal early** (AfroTech coverage references **Talent Infusion** / resume book) so employers can find you before the expo and conversations start warmer — not only day-of scanning.

**[GEM]** **Apply before the booth when possible**, then use booth time to ask about timeline and role nuance; signals preparation and saves queue time for real questions (AfroTech sophomore roadmap).

**[GEM]** **Ask recruiters about “special” or personalized application links** vs. the general applicant pool; multiple sources describe a tighter track when recruiters issue a direct link (AfroTech student article — still **verify wording** with recruiters; don’t promise universal behavior).

**[GEM]** **Network horizontally with other students** at large community events; peers surface invite-only dinners, sessions, and “what’s worth your hour” intel you won’t see on the official grid (AfroTech — student + first-timer articles).

**[GEM]** **Pre-conference LinkedIn**: personalized connection requests or engaging with a panelist’s recent post **before** the event signals intent (AfroTech — HR leader quote).

**[GEM]** **ColorStack virtual summit**: share the **attendance graphic** on LinkedIn pre-event; use org **resume review** Slack; **camera on** for intros and recruiter 1:1s even if you go camera-off later.

**[GEM]** **Plan with buffers**: structured agenda **plus** open slots for spontaneous conversations and recovery — goal is a few high-quality outcomes, not attending every session (AfroTech — Mercedes).

**[GEM]** **Conversation starters that aren’t “so what jobs do you have?”** e.g. highlight of the conference so far, best advice heard here, what panel they’d design — reduces transactional feel (AfroTech — Mercedes list).

**[GEM]** **NSBE-style floor prep**: **7–10** target companies, know mission and **open roles**; push yourself to **one new meaningful relationship per day**; authenticity + name memory + follow-up (Google/NSBE45).

**[GEM]** **Follow-up with specificity** within a short window (same week): reference the topic you discussed; prioritize **fewer, deeper** follow-ups over performative blast (AfroTech — Barnett reflection + Mercedes template pattern).

**Supporting** — Arrive early for shorter lines and fresher recruiters (general fair guides). **Supporting** — Bring resume copies, padfolio, pen; business card or QR to portfolio is optional differentiator (student posts). **Supporting** — Virtual/hybrid: test bandwidth, professional on-camera baseline, timezone discipline (scaffold already flags this). **Supporting** — Balance expo with panels/learning; even if not actively job hunting, use floor for **market calibration** (AfroTech — Barnett).

**[FACT CHECK — high volatility]** Any named **AfroTech** year, **Houston** dates, **ticket** names (e.g. Premiere), **35,000** attendance, **2026 Nov 2–6** copy from blog posts — **confirm on official conference + registration** before publishing. **NSBE** year, city, app names (e.g. attendee guide), and **12,000+** historical attendance — **confirm on [convention.nsbe.org](https://convention.nsbe.org/)**. **ColorStack** sponsor count, **50+** employers, **1,200+** vs **1,700+** attendees — **confirm on current ColorStack/summit pages**.

## RECOMMENDED STRUCTURE

**Opening hook:** Same time on the floor can yield tote bags or a short list of real conversations — the difference is a plan and follow-through.

**Section 1: Before — research, targets, and visibility** — Cap target employers at what you can know cold; portal resume upload and pre-applies; community-specific pre-event moves (LinkedIn graphic + Slack resume support for ColorStack; pre-connects for large summits).

**Section 2: During — floor, booths, and people** — Recruiter vs engineer questions; horizontal student networking; optional private events/links; virtual camera/on-camera intros; when to skip the longest line.

**Section 3: After — follow-up ladder** — 24–48h personalized messages, reference the thread, tie to application status; maintain a small “warm” list.

**Section 4: Budget, access, and formats** — Student discounts, travel grants, chapter/sponsor programs — **only name programs with [FACT CHECK]**; hybrid/virtual hygiene.

**The mistake:** Treating the event as a resume distribution sprint instead of a **few intentional relationships + portal/apply discipline**.

**Close:** One **24-hour** action: export a 5–7 company list with one talking point each + draft one follow-up template.

# Networking System for Introverted Students

## Editor meta

- Silent-read word budget: about 900–1,100 words.
- Voice: `.cursor/rules/navigate-writing.mdc`. Specific beats > “meet people.”
- Volatility **low**.

## Opening (replace with friction-led prose)

Introversion is not an excuse to ghost your own career. It is a constraint you design around like any other.

## Energy accounting

- Events in batches, recovery blocks, when to leave without shame.

## One prep habit that removes cold opens

- Two facts about org or person, one real question, no script cosplay.

## In-room moves that do not require working the whole floor

- Small circles, follow-up with one person you actually liked talking to.

## Online networking without spam

- Commenting with substance, DMs with context.

## Maintaining loose ties

- Lightweight check-ins that do not read transactional.

## When networking is the wrong tool

- Skills gap vs contact gap: honest triage.

- Name concrete campus or city events only when verified for term/date.

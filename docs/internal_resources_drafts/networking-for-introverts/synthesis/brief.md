KEY FINDINGS

[GEM] **Weekly rhythm (UX anchor):** Design the default week as **one meaningful networking action** — e.g., one follow-up email or LinkedIn note with a specific reference, **or** one micro-goal at a single event block (not “work the whole floor”). Matches introvert energy math and still compounds; aligns with “4–6 meaningful conversations” style caps from practitioner event guides rather than endless mingling.

[GEM] **AfroTech-style prep:** Before a big community conference, **set a realistic goal per slice of the day** and **pre-write 2–3 open questions**; expect connections in **lounges and happy hours**, not only panels (AfroTech sourcing). Pair with **intentional listening** to find shared goals — reduces performative small talk.

[GEM] **Visibility for underrepresented students:** In **short networking windows**, **state what you’re building and what you want next** (skills, internship track, project stack). AfroTech/DEI practitioner framing: **overt self-promotion helps Black/brown talent stay top-of-mind** when time with a contact is scarce — reframe as clarity, not bragging.

[GEM] **ColorStack virtual summit moves:** **Post on LinkedIn that you’re attending** before Stacked Up Summit; **refresh resume + LinkedIn** so links are one tap away; in Zoom-style intros, **lead with camera on for the introduction** even if you turn off later — lowers friction for recruiters and peer breakout rooms.

[GEM] **Program-first networking:** Treat **ColorStack Slack**, **Code2040 cohort events**, **MLT seminars/Tech Trek**, and **SEO Tech Developer / Scholars** touchpoints as **lower-cold starts** — you’re networking inside a shared mission and often with a buddy cohort; use those spaces to practice the same follow-up habits before you rely on giant expo halls.

[GEM] **Outreach mechanics (Belcak / Cultivated Culture):** **Follow up** on unanswered messages; practitioner data/storytelling emphasizes that **most people stop after one touch** and that **multiple polite touchpoints** change outcomes. For introverts, this turns conversation into **async, schedulable work** instead of live charisma.

[GEM] **In-room introvert tactics:** **Arrive early** when floors are calmer; seek **structured rotations** (speed networking, office hours) where ends are timed; **map quiet zones** for recovery; optionally bring **one agreed “safe person”** check-in — reduces paralysis at NEU-adjacent career fairs and national conferences alike.

[GEM] **Play to depth:** **One thoughtful follow-up** referencing something they said beats ten shallow handshakes; **public writing or substantive comments** (student-voice async paths) build reputation without cocktail energy.

Supporting point — **Branch out once per event:** AfroTech guidance to **not only cling to the friends you came with** — for introverts, translate to **one solo hello** or **one new lunch seat** per day, not full abandonment of your crew.

Supporting point — **Start before posting season:** Student CS networking posts stress **relationship timing** — light touchpoints across fall/spring beat a panic burst when applications open.

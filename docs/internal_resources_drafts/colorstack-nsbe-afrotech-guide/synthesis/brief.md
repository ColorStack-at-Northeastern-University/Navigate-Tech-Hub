KEY FINDINGS

[GEM] **Align LinkedIn to org applications before you submit.** ColorStack explicitly requires profile details (school, major, grad year) to **match** the application; verified LinkedIn called out as a plus — treat profile hygiene as a gate, not an afterthought. Source: ColorStack member requirements.

[GEM] **Upload to the AfroTech resume book early** so employers can find you before the floor opens; pair with a **lightweight agenda + buffer blocks** so rigid scheduling doesn’t crowd out high-leverage spontaneous conversations. Sources: AfroTech first-time tips (Mercedes); official first-time page (app/schedule).

[GEM] **Use supplied conversation prompts** (e.g., highlight of the conference, dream panel, best advice heard) to lead with curiosity instead of performance; pair with **1–2 intentional follow-ups** rather than superficial mass blasts — both expert and student sources stress intentionality. Source: AfroTech first-time tips.

[GEM] **If your campus has no NSBE chapter, you can still join At-Large** and get national benefits while you explore ColorStack/Code2040 — reduces “my school has nothing” as an excuse. Source: NSBE collegiate page.

[GEM] **Treat major conferences as learning + market scans in year one** — Barnett’s recap: expo time clarified what peers at similar levels were doing and compensation bands **before** feeling technical-interview-ready; sets realistic expectations for first AfroTech without implying everyone should recruit on day one. Source: AfroTech first-time tips.

**ColorStack’s Stacked Up is framed as early relationship-building with partners** (“before recruiting season begins”) — for first-years, that implies **showing up for community signal and reps**, not postponing engagement until you have a finished portfolio. Source: ColorStack summit recap.

**Code2040 Fellows** = cohort + mentor + structured internship support + equity curriculum; timelines are **windowed** (site lists typical May–June cycle — article draft must **re-check dates** annually). Newsletter/email list for “when applications open.” Source: Code2040 programs.

**NSBE national membership year Aug 1–July 31** — joining late in cycle affects convention pricing/benefits; mention **renewal timing** if students care about national events. Source: NSBE collegiate page.

RECOMMENDED STRUCTURE
Opening hook: **First-year FOMO** — seeing peers post summits/conferences while you’re unsure what you’re “allowed” to do without internships or a polished GitHub.

Section 1: **Map the ecosystems (CS-centric vs STEM-wide vs conference)** — ColorStack vs NSBE vs AfroTech vs optional Code2040/MLT; who each serves; **first-year realistic wins** (relationships, information, motivation).

Section 2: **Join and stack early** — application hygiene (LinkedIn match), chapter vs At-Large, newsletters/deadlines; **don’t wait for “ready.”**

Section 3: **Conference playbook (AfroTech-style)** — resume book, app/agenda + buffers, floor vs sessions balance, prompts, follow-up SLA, **year-one “learn the market” permission**.

Section 4: **After the event / semester rhythm** — Slack/community upkeep, one coffee chat or async intro per month, tie-in to Navigate’s LinkedIn guide if needed.

The mistake: **treating these orgs as logos on a résumé** instead of **repeat touchpoints** (prep → show up → follow up → stay in Slack/newsletters).

Close: **24-hour action** — pick **one** org to apply or renew + **one** profile fix (LinkedIn alignment) + **one** date hold or newsletter signup for the next major program window.

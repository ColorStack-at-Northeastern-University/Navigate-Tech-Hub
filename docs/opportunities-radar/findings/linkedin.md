## Notes for human reviewer
- SOAR Program (Strategy, Operations & Analytics Rotational) is **indefinitely paused** per LinkedIn's official page — do not promote to students.
- LinkedIn First Play 2027 cycle application window expected November 2026 based on 2026 cycle timing (Nov 24 – Dec 7, 2025). This is extremely early — students need to know in October so they don't miss the ~2-week window.
- Possibilities in Tech Scholarship is explicitly selection-neutral on race/gender (legal protection, not a DEI eligibility signal) — it targets financial need via FAFSA SAI alongside demonstrated tech passion. Still highly relevant for NEU Black/Latinx students who often have higher financial need.
- REACH Apprenticeship does not require a CS degree and targets self-taught/non-traditional coders — relevant for students who have built projects outside of class but might not yet have a strong GPA or formal credentials.
- No LinkedIn-specific HBCU, URM, or Black/Latinx named fellowship found. LinkedIn's programs are broadly open with financial-need components rather than demographic targeting.
- Associate Product Manager (APM) Program and Legal Summer Program also exist (careers.linkedin.com/pathways-programs) for students interested in product/legal tracks — not in scope for CS-focused radar but worth noting.

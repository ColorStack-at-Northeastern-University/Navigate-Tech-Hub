## Programs found

No qualifying named programs run directly by Atlassian found. Atlassian runs a general summer internship (12 weeks, US: May–September) and a graduate program ("Gradlassian") targeting penultimate and final-year students. Neither is a dedicated named pipeline for freshmen/sophomores or underrepresented students.

**Note on StackedUp Summit**: Atlassian is a corporate sponsor of the ColorStack StackedUp Summit (a free biannual summit for Black/Latinx CS students), but this is a ColorStack-owned event, not an Atlassian program. Atlassian recruiters attend and source candidates there. No 2026 summit date confirmed from search results.
